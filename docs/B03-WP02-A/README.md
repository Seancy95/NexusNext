# B03-WP02-A — Independent Investigative Capability Evaluation Design

**Status:** WP02-A-R01 corrected evaluation design produced for independent architectural and methodological review
**Implementation:** Not Implemented
**Benchmark execution:** Not Run
**Hidden scenarios:** not created or stored in this repository
**Predecessor inspected:** B03-WP01-R01 delivery `94407e66ac9b2640c1d7b05f74c613f8510eea55`

This package specifies how to answer:

> Does a bounded Nexus Next deterministic investigation produce materially better investigative findings than a competent conventional deterministic analytics solution, under equivalent evidence access and resource constraints?

An unfavourable or inconclusive Nexus result is valid. The design does not assume advantage.

## Deliverables

1. `01_EVALUATION_SPECIFICATION.md` — stages, experimental unit, fairness and judgments.
2. `02_SCENARIO_AND_GROUND_TRUTH.md` — scenario generation, protected truth and mandatory cases.
3. `03_SCORING_AND_VALUE_FRAMEWORK.md` — all-output assertion scoring, denominators, statistics, business-value criteria and programme decision matrix.
4. `04_COMPARATOR_SPECIFICATION.md` — competent baseline and independent acceptance.
5. `05_INDEPENDENCE_AND_CONTAMINATION.md` — custody, access, freeze and contamination response.
6. `06_FAILURE_ANALYSIS.md`, `failure_analysis_template.csv` and `assertion_exposure_template.csv` — event-level failure and exposed-assertion history.
7. `07_MINIMAL_WP02_B_SCOPE.md` — smallest proposed implementation, without implementation.
8. `08_RISKS_ASSUMPTIONS_AND_DECISIONS.md` — status-labelled risks and open decisions.
9. `09_WP02_B_APPROVAL_CHECKLIST.md` — explicit approval gate.
10. `requirements_traceability.csv` — requirement-to-design mapping.
11. `CHANGELOG_R01.md` — targeted correction record and readiness recommendation.

All numerical thresholds and business-value criteria are **Proposed** and must be approved before development or hidden evaluation. The corrected proposal uses 80 distinct scenarios for the assumed overall paired effect and 100 for the full programme decision with separate negative/inconclusive strata; pilot evidence may require more. All descriptions of future components are design statements. No analytical algorithm, comparator, scorer, scenario set, access-control service or benchmark infrastructure was implemented or executed.

**Recommendation:** conditional acceptance of the corrected WP02-A-R01 design after independent methodology/statistics review. WP02-B remains **Not Ready** until the G01–G15 approvals, including comparator specification gate C-SPEC, are complete. Hidden evaluation remains prohibited until the separate E01–E07 operational gate passes.
