## Progression decision

WP02-B is **Not Approved** by this design deliverable. Approval requires named sign-off and evidence references for G01–G15. Approval of WP02-B authorises only the bounded implementation and open developer testing in deliverable 7. It does not authorise hidden-set placement in the implementation workspace, benchmark execution, company-data use, Enhanced Mode, finding acceptance, reporting/publication or WP02-C.

The corrected WP02-A-R01 design is recommended for **conditional design acceptance** after independent methodology/statistics approval. WP02-B readiness remains **Not Ready** while any G-item is pending.
