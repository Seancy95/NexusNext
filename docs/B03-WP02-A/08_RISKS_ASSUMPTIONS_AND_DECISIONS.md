# Risks, assumptions and unresolved decisions

## 1. Status vocabulary

- **Approved requirement:** supplied WP02-A instruction or previously accepted authority boundary.
- **Proposed method:** this design's recommended means of satisfying a requirement; requires review.
- **Untested assumption:** premise that has not been empirically established.
- **Unresolved decision:** named authority must decide before the dependent stage.

## 3. Principal risks

| Risk | Consequence | Proposed control | Residual limitation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hidden truth accessible to implementers | Memorisation/contamination invalidates claim | Separate administration, ACLs, logs, input/truth split and retirement | Requires real organisational separation not available in this workspace |
| Comparator deliberately or accidentally weak | Inflated Nexus advantage | Independent ownership, capability fixtures, challenge window and fairness sign-off | Competence remains context-dependent |
| Scenarios mirror one mechanism | False generalisation | Factorial families, independent authors and family-clustered reporting | Synthetic population may still differ from deployment |
| Ground truth confuses mechanism with observable proof | Rewards causal guessing | Separate causal and observable-evidence fields; independent reviewer | Some evidential judgments require adjudication |
| Thresholds chosen after seeing results | Biased success rule | Signed preregistration and versioned deviations | Business value judgment still contestable |
| Composite hides critical error | Unsafe pass | Separate gates and event reporting; zero-tolerance integrity | Absolute gates can reduce statistical flexibility by design |
| Adaptive system gets more opportunity | Unfair comparison | Equal operation and opportunity budgets; measure compute separately | Internal efficiency may be structurally different |
| Fixed comparator cannot answer question | Straw baseline | Multi-round reviewed playbook and same follow-ups | Does not represent every possible human analyst |
| Repeated scenarios counted as independent | False precision | Scenario is sampling unit; clustered intervals | Limited distinct scenarios reduce power |
| Missing/failed runs selectively excluded | Optimistic result | Prospective eligibility; failures remain outcomes | External outages can still cause inconclusive blocks |
| Evaluator subjectivity | Unreliable scoring | Objective annotations, blinded dual scoring sample, adjudication log | Some alternative conclusions require judgment |
| Implementation complexity outweighs gain | Poor investment | Report complexity separately and apply investment rule | Operational cost estimates may be immature |
| WP01 authority inadvertently weakened | Self-promotion/verification failure | Shared controller/verifier and denial regressions | Runtime identity remains a separate future control |
| Synthetic success presented as production readiness | Overclaim | Five separate judgments and scope-limited conclusion | Organisational communication discipline required |

## 4. Untested assumptions

- Paired recall-difference SD is approximately 0.30; the pilot may show that 80 overall or 100 full-decision scenarios are inadequate.
- The chosen synthetic population can represent the operational investigative decisions relevant to the intended claim.
- A competent comparator can be implemented within the shared operation and budget interface without hidden disadvantage.
- The bounded WP02-B method subset can produce a measurable advantage without the full catalogue.
- Independent reviewers can agree sufficiently on observable support, acceptable alternatives and stop points.
- Opportunity-unit charging can equate substantively similar retrievals across different internal strategies.

These assumptions are not findings. Pilot/development evidence may refine the next preregistration but cannot be mixed into confirmatory hidden conclusions.

## 5. Blocking unresolved decisions

WP02-B cannot start until the approval checklist is complete. Hidden scenario creation cannot start until custody, personnel, materiality and ground-truth procedures are approved. Hidden execution cannot start until both implementations, public rubric, scorer, access controls and run plan are frozen. No result may be called independent until the access boundary has evidence.

WP02-A-R01 is recommended for **conditional design acceptance** once an independent methodology/statistics reviewer approves the corrected denominators, sample-size assumptions, thresholds and business-value matrix. WP02-B remains **Not Ready** until every blocking checklist item, including comparator C-SPEC, is approved. This recommendation does not authorise implementation.
