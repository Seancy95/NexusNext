# Reasoning Boundary and Investigative Method Catalogue

## 1. Governing boundary

**Approved requirement:** Baseline is a deterministic investigation engine. Enhanced extends the same governed architecture with strictly local semantic interpretation and contextual reasoning. Baseline is defined by inspectable methods and decisions, not by the absence of an LLM. General statistical, anomaly, relationship, contradiction, hypothesis and multi-round capabilities belong to Baseline.

Enhanced contributions are proposals. Enhanced cannot independently retrieve evidence, recalculate an authoritative metric when an approved deterministic calculation exists, approve knowledge, accept a finding, change canonical evidence, or bypass report/publication controls.

## 3. Method contract common to all entries

Every method has a stable ID/version, declared mathematical assumptions, accepted schemas, deterministic configuration, required domain rules, evidence-reference inputs, result schema, failure states, cost function and acceptance fixtures. The method cannot select its own authority rules or accept its output as a finding. `EvidenceRef` means a local reference to a frozen evidence unit with source, scope, snapshot, digest, acquisition/expiry and lineage; it is not a raw payload in the method result.

## 4. Investigative method catalogue — analytical definition

|---|---|---|---|---|
| M01 Time-series analysis | Quantify level/rate change over time; “when did performance move?” | Consistent metric definition, ordered periods, comparable population, known missing intervals | Aggregate by governed period; compute absolute/relative change, rolling summaries only when window preregistered; preserve gaps | Series observations + period/population refs → period metrics, deltas, coverage and refs |
| M02 Distribution comparison | Determine whether centre, spread or tails changed rather than relying on averages | Individual or valid grouped observations; same units/population rules; adequate sample | Quantiles, ECDF/histogram with fixed bins, robust location/spread; approved two-sample test/effect size where assumptions hold | Two scoped populations + refs → quantiles, effect sizes/test diagnostics and refs |
| M03 Trend and change-point analysis | Distinguish sustained trend from a one-period fluctuation; locate structural change candidates | Sufficient ordered periods, stable measurement, seasonality metadata | Predeclared regression/non-parametric trend; candidate change point from registered algorithm with minimum segment and multiplicity policy | Series/config + refs → trend estimate, candidate points, intervals/diagnostics and refs |
| M04 Outlier detection | Find unusual records or groups for review | Comparable features, quality-checked units, declared peer group | Robust univariate fences/MAD or registered multivariate distance; fixed threshold and missing-value rule | Feature rows + refs → outlier leads, scores, threshold and contributing refs |
| M05 Statistical anomaly detection | Detect departures from expected temporal/peer behaviour | Historical/peer baseline, exposure denominator, uncertainty model, drift/seasonality controls | Prediction/control interval or registered residual test; adjust repeated comparisons; emit anomaly hypothesis only | Observed/baseline series + refs → anomaly lead, expected range, p/effect diagnostics and refs |
| M06 KPI and SLA calculation | Calculate governed operational measure or contractual performance | Approved definition, population/exclusions, events, calendar, target and effective period | Versioned aggregation or business-time calculation; explicit numerator/denominator, pauses, boundaries and rounding | Typed records + rule/calendar refs → value, components, classifications, exclusions and refs |
| M07 Population segmentation | Locate cohorts contributing to aggregate movement | Approved dimensions or discoverable typed attributes, adequate cell sizes, stable membership | Partition by declared fields; compute within-segment and mix changes; suppress/qualify small cells; no causal label | Population + dimension refs → segment table, contribution candidates, coverage and refs |

## 6. Statistical governance

Method registrations must declare sampling unit, population/cohort construction, missing-data treatment, independence/exchangeability assumptions, repeated-measure structure, effect-size definition, uncertainty method, multiplicity handling, minimum data, deterministic seed where relevant, and invalid-assumption behavior. Exploratory analysis is labelled. Repeated observations are not independent units. A statistically unusual change is not a causal result. Numerical thresholds, materiality tiers and acceptable false-positive/false-negative bounds remain unresolved until approved for each use or preregistered benchmark category.
